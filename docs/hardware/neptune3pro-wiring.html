ESP32-S3 · ESP3D-TFT · stock Marlin · USB-B

Wire the ESP3D-TFT touchscreen to the Neptune 3 Pro

The Guition ESP32-S3-4848S040 runs ESP3D-TFT but cannot be a USB host: its USB pins drive the touch controller and the RGB bus. The Neptune 3 Pro only takes G-code on its USB-B port. A spare ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1 sits between them as a USB-host bridge. The printer keeps its stock firmware and its stock screen.

01

Parts

Everything is off the shelf. No soldering on any board.

Guition ESP32-S3-4848S040

4.0" 480×480 touch panel, 16 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM. Runs ESP3D-TFT (target ESP32S3_4848S040C).

ESP32-S3-DevKitC-1

Any S3 DevKitC with both a USB and a UART port. Runs tools/usb_host_bridge.

USB A-to-B printer cable

The printer's USB-B port to the bridge.

USB OTG adapter

USB-A female to the DevKitC's connector (USB-C or micro-USB, depending on board revision).

P1 pigtail + 3 jumpers

4-pin 1.25 mm cable (usually in the display's box) and three Dupont leads to the DevKitC header.

Two 5V USB supplies

One for the display (1 A or more, it drives the backlight), one for the bridge.

02

Wiring: where every wire goes

One harness of three wires between the bridge and the display, then one USB cable to the printer. TX goes to RX and RX goes to TX; the grounds are joined.

Harness A: the bridge to the display (3 wires)

!

The DevKitC has two USB ports. The printer goes in the one marked USB (native ESP32-S3, GPIO19/20). The one marked UART (or COM) is power and the bridge's log console. Swapped, nothing enumerates and the LED stays blue.

03

Power: who feeds what

Each board runs from its own USB supply. Nothing on the data path carries power.

BoardPowered fromNotes
DisplayUSB-C, 5V ≥ 1 AThe P1 +5V input has no bulk capacitance and causes resets. Never power USB-C and P1 together: they back-feed.
Bridge"UART" USB portAny 5V USB supply or a PC (then you also get the log console).
Printer CH340GPrinter 3V3 railThe bridge does not need to supply USB VBUS. The bridge connects when the printer powers on.
✕

Do not let anything toggle DTR. On the Neptune 3 Pro mainboard the CH340G's DTR reaches the STM32 reset through C33. A PC opening the port, or a host that sets DTR, resets the printer and kills a running print. The bridge leaves DTR and RTS alone (CONFIG_BRIDGE_ASSERT_DTR is off).

04

Flash both boards

From the repo (joshuawowk/ESP3D-TFT), ESP-IDF 5.1.5. Flash the display before wiring P1, or unplug P1 to reflash: the bridge's TX shares the display's RX line with its CH340.

display · ESP3D-TFT
idf.py -DESP32S3_HMI43V3=OFF -DESP32S3_4848S040C=ON set-target esp32s3 idf.py build idf.py -p /dev/ttyUSB0 -b 460800 flash # 921600 corrupts on the CH340
bridge · tools/usb_host_bridge
cd tools/usb_host_bridge idf.py set-target esp32s3 idf.py build idf.py -p /dev/ttyUSB1 flash monitor # through the DevKitC "UART" port

Once running, the bridge's native USB port is in host mode and no longer shows up on a PC. Hold BOOT and tap RESET to flash through it again.

restore the display's factory firmware
esptool.py --chip esp32s3 -p /dev/ttyUSB0 -b 460800 write_flash 0x0 factory-backup.bin
05

Bring-up checks

In order. Each step proves one link before the next depends on it.

1

Display alone

Splash, then the main screen, upright, correct colours, taps land under your finger. Without a printer the temperatures stay empty.

2

Bridge LED blue

Powered, waiting for a USB device. On a v1.0 DevKitC the LED is on GPIO48 and stays dark; set BRIDGE_LED_GPIO=48.

3

Printer on, LED green

The CH340G enumerated and the bridge opened it at 115200 8N1. The console shows Printer USB device opened then Bridge active.

4

Temperatures on the display

ESP3D-TFT polls with M105/M114; hotend and bed readings appear. Every 10 s the bridge logs byte counts in both directions.

bridge console (UART port, 115200)
I (312) bridge: ESP3D-TFT USB host bridge: UART1 TX=GPIO17 RX=GPIO18 @115200, USB @115200, DTR untouched I (322) bridge: Waiting for the printer USB device... I (2841) bridge: Printer USB device opened I (2851) bridge: Bridge active I (12322) bridge: printer->tft 1284 B, tft->printer 96 B, dropped (no printer) 0 B

Sample log; timestamps and byte counts vary.
